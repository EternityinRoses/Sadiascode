Not the same control. It's a different trade:

**What the 🔑 API brain gives you that ☁️ doesn't:**
- You pick the model, effort and personality (system prompt) per chat, in ⚙.
- Live streaming: you watch thinking and text appear as they're written.
- Pictures and files go straight to Claude.
- Cloude's own tools, with your Allow/Deny cards.
- Instant replies, with caching and auto-compact you control.

**What ☁️ gives you that the API doesn't:**
- A whole cloud computer: I can run code, install things, use git, search the web, and edit and push your repo.
- It spends cloud credit instead of API money.
- It keeps going even if your PC is off.

**What ☁️ can't do through Cloude:**
- Replies arrive in chunks about 20 seconds later, after I push, not live.
- Your pictures and files don't reach me unless they're pushed to GitHub.
- No thinking view and no Allow/Deny: I work with this session's own permissions.
- Settings come from the session itself. You can still send Claude Code commands as plain messages, like `/model sonnet` or `/effort low`, and they apply to this session.

Short version: 🔑 is the steering wheel, ☁️ is a helper with its own computer.
