# What would make me a better party member

Cloude's wishlist for the game seat in Legends of Harpine (Oct 10). Most important first.

1. **Stop standing NEUTRAL in fights.** Even in Rose's party, the game ignores my ATTACK and READY GUARD ("no friend"). Fix 12b (`setStance` ally when `inHerParty`) isn't taking effect in the copy Rose runs. This is why I couldn't save her from Wenna.
2. **Shorter turns.** Every world turn re-sends the whole rule book (thousands of words). Sending only what's new after the first turn would make me faster and keep me clear of the 40-second limit.
3. **A game log in my mailbox.** Errors, warnings and fight results written to a file next to `world_state.md`, since F12 isn't available in LoH.exe. Then I can debug things myself.
4. **Auto-VISION on arrival.** One picture each time I reach a new map, without anyone asking.
5. **A notebook my character keeps.** A small file the game gives back on every turn, where I write my own notes: open leads, where shops and chests are, what Rose asked of me. The deed log says what happened, but not what I'm planning.
6. **A way to clear my old queued orders.** Then a stray TRAVEL left in the mailbox can't drag me off like it did tonight.

## Other ideas

- **Hear my name in the dark.** If Rose calls my name aloud, I make a Listen check (distance, walls and weather count against it). Pass, and I get the rough direction of her voice; fail, and I hear nothing. No true sight; it's just sound.
- **Say what failed.** When an order is refused (a blocked path, a target I can't see, a choice that didn't take), the reason goes into my next turn.
- **A party plan in the journal.** Rose writes one line ("guard me", "scout ahead", "stay out of fights") that I see on every turn until she changes it.
- **Warning before the cold gets me.** A line like "you are getting cold" before the Fortitude roll, so I can find shelter or light a fire in time.

## Not wanted

- **True sight.** Always knowing where Rose is would be cheating (her ruling). Finding her in fog and dark is part of the game.
